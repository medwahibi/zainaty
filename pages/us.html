about us

Our legacy

DR. AI LING JEAVONS is a clinician by training, with decades of experience in facial aesthetics and skin health.

Her work has always treated the skin as a biological system. In clinical practice, she worked extensively with regenerative injectables, including polynucleotides [PDRN], witnessing the skin's capacity to restore strength, density, and function when supported at a cellular level.

This insight revealed a contradiction: the most meaningful results required invasive intervention, while everyday skincare prioritised surface change — often at the expense of the skin barrier.

Through advanced delivery research within Korean biotechnology, Dr. Jeavons selected Microspherized PDRN, a refined format designed for improved penetration, stability, and skin compatibility.

This evidence-led, restrained approach became the foundation of Beauty in STEM.

KOREAN FORMULAS ENGINEERED FOR ULTIMATE SKIN FITNESS.

science that works in harmony with skin.

Beauty in STEM approaches skincare as Skin Fitness — the discipline of strengthening the skin through science-led, barrier-respecting formulation.

Rather than overstimulating or overriding natural processes, each product is designed to support cellular resilience, reinforce the barrier, and sustain performance over time.

Central to this philosophy is PDRN — DNA-derived regenerative fragments that are biologically familiar to the skin.

This is skincare developed with biological respect: effective, measured, and built for longevity.

The results validate this approach. Across Dr. Jeavons' clients, improvements in under-eye texture, hydration, and skin reactivity were consistently reported.
